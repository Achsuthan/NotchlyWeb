Music

Every player.
One place.

Apple Music, Spotify, a video in Safari or Chrome — Notchly finds whatever's playing and gives each its own row and controls. The list stays in order, so nothing jumps around under your cursor.

  • GlanceArtwork and a listening mascot in the collapsed notch.
  • ControlPlay, pause and skip — or ±15s for videos and podcasts.
  • JumpTap any row to bring that app forward.

AirDrop

Drop it
on the notch.

Start dragging from Finder, the Desktop or Mail and the notch opens into a drop zone before you even get there. Prefer to gather first? Fill the AirDrop shelf, review the thumbnails, and send it all at once.

  • Quick dropLet go and the AirDrop picker opens straight away.
  • ShelfCollect files with QuickLook previews and a total size.
  • NativeUses macOS's own AirDrop — nothing leaves your Mac any other way.

Claude Code

Your agents,
in plain sight.

Connect Claude Code once and every session — in any terminal or editor — reports in. Follow progress, approve tool calls and answer Claude's questions right from the notch.

  • Every sessionSeveral projects and terminals at once, each with a live status.
  • Answer in placeAllow, deny, always allow — or pick an answer to Claude's question.
  • Right tabJumps to the exact Terminal, iTerm2 or VS Code window it came from.

The details

Small touches.
Big difference.

Liquid Glass

On macOS 26, expanded cards fade from the black of the camera housing into real Liquid Glass. Earlier versions get a matching translucent blur.

⌘

Every Mac

No notch? Notchly draws one at the top of your screen, so Mac mini, iMac and Studio users get it too.

🔒

Stays on your Mac

Claude Code talks to Notchly over a local-only connection. No account, no cloud.

↩︎

Leaves no trace

Disconnect from the menu bar and Notchly removes only its own hooks — your settings are backed up first.

Your priority, remembered

Tabs tucked beside the camera let you choose what the notch is about — Claude, Music or AirDrop. Notchly remembers, and only an agent waiting on you can interrupt.

Say hello to Notchly.

Open the disk image and drag Notchly to Applications. That's it.
